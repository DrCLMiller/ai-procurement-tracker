Effective dates, compliance deadlines and sunsets across all tracked rules, soonest first. Past dates are listed below for reference.

How to use this. This is a research and planning aid for procurement teams. It is not legal advice. Dates and statuses change often, especially proposed rules and state laws under federal challenge, so check the linked sources before relying on a date. Updates are proposed by an automated weekly review and published only after an editor approves them. See something out of date? Use Report a change.

Status categories: In force = binding and currently applies. Phased = enacted, with obligations still to start. Proposed or pending = draft, rulemaking or framework not yet implemented. Guidance = non-binding guidance, model clauses or voluntary standards.
